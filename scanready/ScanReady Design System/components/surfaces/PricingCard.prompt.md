A pricing tier. Serif price, mint feature checks; the featured tier is the only place the mint glow is allowed.

```jsx
<PricingCard name="Free" price="$0" description="For side projects." features={["1 project","Community support"]} cta="Start free" />
<PricingCard name="Pro" price="$29" featured features={["Unlimited projects","Priority support","Custom domain"]} />
```
