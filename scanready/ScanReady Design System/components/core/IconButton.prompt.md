Icon-only control for toolbars / compact actions. Always pass a `label` for accessibility.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Search"><img src="assets/icons/search.svg" width="20" /></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="primary" shape="circle" label="New"><img src="assets/icons/arrow-right.svg" width="20" /></IconButton>
```

Variants `primary | secondary | ghost`; sizes `sm(32) | md(40) | lg(44)`; shape `rounded | circle`.
