# Brand Name Shortlist (replaces WIP title "ScanReady")

> Research date: 2026-07-05. Two research passes: German/bilingual wordplay + English-first/international.
> Domain status via whois/DENIC lookups (heuristic). **Before committing: run the finalists through DPMA
> register (Nice classes 35/41/42) and EUIPO, and re-verify domains at registration time.**

## Why replace "ScanReady"

- "Scan" naming is mentally owned by **Jobscan** (jobscan.co, established ATS scanner). Any "Scan-X" resume tool reads derivative.
- The "8-second scan" claim the name was built on is already retired (DACH evidence ~43s).

## The three real candidates

| Name | Angle | .de | .com | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bewerbungsfertig** | German-authentic: "application-ready", the literal end-state promise | free | free | Strongest German-insider signal, matches the new founder story. Harder for EN-searching users to say/spell; descriptive, so weaker trademark registrability. |
| **CV Broadsheet** | Editorial-brand: names the print metaphor the whole design system is built on (masthead, broadsheet band, typesetting theater) | free | free | Distinctive and design-coherent; "CV" catches EN search intent. Doesn't say "Germany" without the tagline. |
| **German CV Co.** | Search-forward: literal match on "german cv" queries | free | free | Best SEO/value clarity; least distinctive as a brand mark. Safe fallback. |

Recommendation: pick by strategy. If the founder story ("built by a German") becomes the brand spine, **Bewerbungsfertig** is the name that only we can credibly carry; pair it with an English tagline ("Application-ready for Germany") so EN users parse it. If the editorial design is the spine, **CV Broadsheet**.

## Runners-up

- **Recruiter Ink** (.de/.com free) – best pure editorial-serif fit, but zero Germany signal.
- **Anschreibenwerk** (.de/.com free) – craft/workshop feel, no collisions, but long and EN-hostile.
- **Klarmappe** (.de/.com free) – short, "honest folder"; soft root-collision with mappen.ai (job-verification portal). DPMA-check before use.
- **Unterlagenwerk** (.de/.com free) – covers the whole Bewerbungsmappe; serious tone.
- **Scanfertig** (.de/.com free) – continuity bridge from ScanReady; inherits the "Scan" problem.

## Avoid (collisions found)

- **Mappe/Mappen-root names** – mappen.ai is a live job-verification portal.
- **Klarlauf** – existing wastewater-treatment term.
- **RecruitLens/RecruiterLens** – recruitlens.io is a live AI resume-analysis product.
- **Anything "Scan-", "Resume-X", "CV-wizard/builder"-patterned** – owned by Jobscan / resume.io / Zety cluster; reads as a knockoff or an Abofalle mill (the exact thing we position against).
- **Dear Recruiter, Apply in Germany, GetGermanReady, TypeGerman, Masthead, Werdegang, Klarschrift** – .com taken/squatted or descriptive-term crowding.
- **Bare "Lebenslauf-X"** – dozens of German template sites crowd the term (lebenslauf.de, XING's lebenslauf.com, ...). No trademark issue, but zero differentiation.
