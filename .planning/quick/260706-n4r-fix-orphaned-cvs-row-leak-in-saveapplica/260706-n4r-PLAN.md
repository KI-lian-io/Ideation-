<objective>
Fix an orphaned-row data leak in `saveApplicationPackage()` (scanready/src/lib/account.ts). Today it inserts a `cvs` row, then an `application_packages` row. When the package insert is rejected by the `enforce_package_limit()` DB trigger (free tier: 1 package), the already-inserted `cvs` row is never removed, so every blocked save leaks one full-CV-text row.

Purpose: prevent unbounded accumulation of orphaned full-CV-text rows on free-tier users who click "save" past their limit. This is a privacy/storage-hygiene issue on a stateless-by-default product where retained CV text is a deliberate opt-in.

Output: a minimal best-effort cleanup delete in the `pkgError` branch, plus a unit test proving the cleanup fires on a limit error while keeping the SaveResult contract unchanged.

Scope guard (out of scope, do NOT do): transactions, RPC rewrites, new abstractions, refactors, changing the SaveResult shape, or touching any other helper in account.ts.
</objective>
