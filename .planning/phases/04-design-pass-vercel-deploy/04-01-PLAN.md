<objective>
Establish the brand foundation (light-only surface, deep-blue trust accent token, Lora serif headings, real metadata) and perform the D-01 route split: move the existing working tool flow from `/` to `/app` and free `/` for the landing (built in Plan 02). Retrofit the tool UI and its components to the new identity — strip all dark-mode variants, upgrade the tool heading to the 28px tier, remove flat-surface-violating shadows, and close the Phase-1 WCAG-AA contrast gap.

Purpose: Every later plan (landing, deploy) depends on the brand tokens existing and the tool living at its own route. This is the load-bearing first increment. Per D-05 the backend is frozen — zero prompt/schema/API logic changes; this is identity + routing only.
Output: A light-only, branded app whose full tool flow works end-to-end at `/app`, with `/` freed for the landing.
</objective>
